Everything you need to know before your first Pawly visit, in one calm little place.

How do I book an appointment?

You can call us on 01273 555 014 or use the booking link on our home page. Our reception team will find a time that works for you and your pet.

What should I bring to the first visit?

Please bring any previous medical records, current medication details and a list of questions. A favourite blanket or treat can help your pet feel at home.

How early should I arrive?

Arriving ten minutes early gives your pet time to settle in and gives us time to complete their registration without rushing.

Do you see nervous or anxious pets?

Absolutely. We use low-stress handling, quiet waiting options and plenty of time. Tell us when booking so we can plan the gentlest visit possible.

Can I ask about costs before treatment?

Yes. We explain recommended options and expected costs before proceeding. If a plan changes, we will always talk it through with you first.

What should I do in an emergency?

Call us immediately on 01273 555 014. If it is outside our opening hours, our voicemail will direct you to the nearest emergency service.
