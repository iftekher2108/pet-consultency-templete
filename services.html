our services

Everything they need to feel their best.

Good veterinary care is a conversation, not a conveyor belt. We take time to understand the whole pet in front of us.

Wellness visits

Head-to-tail examinations, weight checks and age-appropriate prevention to keep small changes from becoming big worries.

Vaccinations

Personalised vaccination plans for puppies, kittens, adults and travellers, with gentle handling at every step.

Dental care

Oral health checks, dental cleaning and practical home advice for fresher breath and a happier smile.

Nutrition advice

Friendly guidance for weight, allergies, life-stage diets and the treats that make sense for your pet.

Diagnostics

Thoughtful blood work, imaging and screening when your pet needs a closer look and a quick answer.

Senior support

Comfort-focused plans for older pets, from mobility checks to ongoing condition management.
